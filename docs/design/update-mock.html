Flow. Sparkle checks once a day. A new version downloads in the background (1) and is verified and unpacked (2) without asking anything; the notch only shows it in the ears. When it is ready (3) one strip appears with Later · Restart. Later keeps a teal lamp on the ears and a "Restart to update" key in the open panel (7); the strip does not come back the same day. During a recording (4) there is no Restart: it waits for the call to end. Restart (5) shows a short "Restarting" while Crenel quits; Sparkle replaces the app in 1–2 seconds while it is closed, so there is nothing to show then. When Crenel opens again (6) it says what version you are on and offers What's new.
