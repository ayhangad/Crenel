# Crenel 1.0

The first release. Crenel lives in the notch: your calendar, dictation, tasks and meeting
notes in one place. Recordings and meeting notes are stored on your Mac.

- **Calendar in the notch.** The next event and how long until it starts, or how long is
  left in the one you're in. Join a call with one key; invites waiting for a reply are marked.
- **Dictation.** Hold fn (🌐) and talk; the words go where your cursor is. Speech is turned
  into text on your Mac and never saved as audio. Long dictation grows into a live view, and
  you can take it back with Undo.
- **Tasks.** Type one line: "tomorrow 3pm", "next Monday", "every Monday" are understood as
  you write. Tasks live in a "Crenel" list in Apple Reminders, so they're on your iPhone too.
- **Meeting notes.** Crenel offers to record Zoom and Google Meet calls, then writes a
  transcript with speakers, a summary, decisions and action items. Export as Markdown, PDF
  or plain text.
- **Your keys, your choice.** Apple Speech transcribes meeting audio on your Mac by default. Choosing
  ElevenLabs sends it the combined recording. Apple’s model handles notes locally. Choosing
  OpenAI or Anthropic sends transcript text to that provider. Claude Code sends text to
  Anthropic using your existing sign-in. OpenAI and Anthropic API providers can also clean up
  dictated text.
- **Updates.** Crenel checks for updates itself and installs them when you're not on a call.
