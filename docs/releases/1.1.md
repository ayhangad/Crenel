# Crenel 1.1

- **Deepgram for meeting transcripts.** Choose Deepgram in Settings › AI › Voice as an
  alternative to ElevenLabs. Transcripts use Nova-3 with speaker labels, in Turkish, English
  or a detected language. Your Deepgram key is checked without uploading audio and kept in
  Keychain, separate from your ElevenLabs key. Choosing Deepgram sends the combined meeting
  recording to Deepgram.
- **Markdown export.** Exported notes no longer name a transcription service, since the
  transcript doesn't record which one wrote it.
