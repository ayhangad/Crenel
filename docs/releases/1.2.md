# Crenel 1.2

- **Your mic is on when a recording starts.** Turn it off in the notch or with the shortcut.
  Muting yourself in Zoom or Google Meet still turns it off, and unmuting turns it back on.
  If Crenel can't read the call app's mute button, it leaves your mic as it is.
- **Record Slack huddles and other calls.** Recording no longer stops with "couldn't verify
  the meeting audio" because the calendar has a Zoom or Meet link, Zoom is open in the
  background, or Chrome is playing something else. Calls in apps Crenel doesn't track
  directly (Slack, Teams, FaceTime) are recorded from all system audio.
- **Cancel and Stop work in the notch.** Deleting a recording, cancelling setup and stopping
  processing are confirmed right in the notch, not in a system alert that could open out of reach.
- **Calendar works right after you allow it.** No relaunch needed; Reminders too.
- **The 🌐 key comes back as soon as you quit.** With fn as the dictation key, quitting
  Crenel or choosing another key restores your "Press 🌐 key to" setting immediately. Before,
  it took a restart.
