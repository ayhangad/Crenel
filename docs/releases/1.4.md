# Crenel 1.4

- **Record anything, any time.** One Record button always sits on the top row of Today,
  with or without a meeting. It also works right after a recording finished, failed or is
  still being processed.
- **Google Meet records without the Chrome extension.** Meet calls are recorded from your
  Mac's audio and your mic, like any other call. Nothing to click in the Meet tab and
  nothing to install in the browser; the extension is retired.
- **Your mic stays out while you're muted.** Muting yourself in Zoom or Google Meet keeps
  your mic out of the recording, as before.
- **Calendar and Reminders show up as soon as you allow them.** Crenel no longer keeps
  showing "Allow" after access was granted until you quit and reopen it.
- **A local tool.** Crenel records what your Mac plays and your mic, and doesn't notify
  anyone on the call. Whether you may record, and asking the others, is up to you.
